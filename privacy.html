Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Greensboro Rodent Control ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses. Handles info when you visit greensbororodentcontrol.com or call us at (844) 635-0403. We've written this in plain language because legal boilerplate doesn't help anyone understand what's really happening.

What we collect and how

When you visit this website

We use standard web analytics (Google Analytics or equivalent) to understand how visitors find and use the site. This collects anonymized data including pages visited, time on page, general geographic region (city-level, not address), referring source. Device/browser type. We do not receive your name, email, or any personally identifying info from this analytics data.

Like most websites, our server logs record IP addresses, request timestamps, and pages accessed. These logs are retained for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes and are not used for marketing.

When you call us

Our phone number routes through a call tracking system (Ringba) that records which marketing source drove your call (search, direct. Referral) and basic call metadata (duration, timestamp, originating number). If you leave a voicemail, that recording is retained until the callback is completed and then deleted.

We do not sell your phone number to third parties. We do not add you to automated marketing lists without your explicit consent. Your number is used solely to return your call and work with service.

When you email us

If you reach us at contact@greensbororodentcontrol.com, we retain your email and the content of your message to respond and work with service. We do not add email contacts to bulk marketing lists.

How we use information

We use the info we collect to:

  • Return phone calls and respond to service inquiries
  • Plan and work with rodent control service
  • Understand which pages and content are useful so we can improve the site
  • Monitor for security issues and unauthorized access

We do not use your info to build advertising profiles, sell to data brokers. Engage in behavioral targeting across other websites.

Cookies

This site uses cookies in two limited ways:

  • Analytics cookies: set by Google Analytics (or equivalent) to tell apart unique visitors and track session behavior. These are anonymized and do not identify you personally.
  • Call tracking cookies: set by Ringba to associate your website visit with a phone call, so we know which marketing source drove your contact. These do not contain personally identifying info.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or social-media tracking tags on this site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. This will not prevent you from viewing the site or calling us.

Third-party services

The following third-party services process data in connection with this website:

  • Google Analytics: website analytics. Google's privacy policy governs their data handling: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Ringba: call tracking and routing. Ringba's privacy policy: ringba.com/privacy-policy.
  • Cloudflare: content delivery and DDoS protection. All site traffic passes through Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

We do not share your personal info with any third parties beyond those listed above. Only to the extent necessary to operate the site and our phone-based service.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained according to the default settings of our analytics provider (usually 26 months). Call logs are retained for up to 12 months for business records. Voicemails are deleted after the callback is completed. Email correspondence is retained for as long as it is relevant to an ongoing or recent service relationship.

Your rights

North Carolina does not now have a complete consumer data privacy law equivalent to California's CCPA or Virginia's CDPA. Still, we are happy to:

  • Tell you what personal info we hold about you, if any
  • Delete any personal info we hold at your request, where technically feasible
  • Correct inaccurate info

To make any of these requests, email us at contact@greensbororodentcontrol.com or call (844) 635-0403.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal info from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal info to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change or as legal requirements evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. We won't notify visitors of minor clarifications, but material changes will be noted prominently.
