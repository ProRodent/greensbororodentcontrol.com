Our process

All in writing.

Most jobs include both trapping and sealing. One or two follow-up visits is standard. Wrap-up comes in a week or two.

  • Service log: Date, technician, bait stations inspected, bait consumption by station, rodent activity noted, and corrective actions taken.
  • Site diagram: Floor-plan diagram showing bait-station placement, numbered to match the service log. Updated when station placement changes.
  • Product data sheets: SDS and product labels for every rodenticide in use at your home, required for health inspections and provided proactively.
  • Activity trend reports: Quarterly summary of bait consumption and rodent activity across all stations, useful for identifying pressure increases before they become infestations.
  • 24-hour records access: We can provide current service records within 24 hours for unannounced health inspections. For accounts under active monthly service, we can often provide same-day.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What documentation do you provide for commercial accounts and health inspections?

Every business account receives a service log documenting each visit, date, technician, bait stations inspected, bait consumption recorded, rodent activity noted. Corrective actions taken. We also provide a site diagram showing bait-station placement and a product data sheet for every rodenticide in use. We can provide this records on 24 hours' notice for unannounced inspections.

Can you service our business without disrupting operations?

Yes. Most business accounts plan service during off-hours, before opening or after closing. For restaurants, that usually means early morning or post-close. For retail and office, it's usually evenings or weekends. We work around your plan, not ours. For accounts requiring daytime service, we work in low-traffic areas and work with your manager to avoid customer-facing spaces during job.

Our business just failed a health inspection for rodents, how fast can you respond?

Same-day. A failed health inspections for rodent activity is a business-closure risk and we treat it as an emergency. Call (844) 635-0403, explain the situation. We'll dispatch a technician and get you records for re-inspections as fast as possible. We've supported re-inspections clearance for downtown Greensboro restaurants and business kitchens, call us first.

Do you offer ongoing scheduled commercial rodent monitoring?

Yes. Ongoing business checking programs include monthly or bi-monthly site visits, bait-station inspections and replenishment, checking log updates. An annual site perimeter assessment. Monthly programs are standard for restaurants, food production, and warehouse work. Bi-monthly is right for offices, retail, and lower-risk business homes. We don't sell quarterly contracts to businesses that need monthly service, that's a common industry practice we deliberately avoid.

How much does commercial rodent control cost for a Greensboro business?

We don't quote commercial rodent control by phone, because the price depends on the property. After a free inspection you get a written quote before any work starts. For commercial rodent control, the quote is shaped mainly by the number of units or spaces, how much of the property is affected, and the visit schedule the site needs.
