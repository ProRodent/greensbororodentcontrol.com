Frequently asked

Common questions

As a landlord, am I legally required to address rodent problems in Greensboro rental properties?

Yes. North Carolina landlords are required under the Home Rental Agreements Act (NCGS § 42-42) to maintain rental premises in a fit and habitable state. This includes keeping the premises free from rodents and insects. Failure to address a documented rodent infestation, after tenant notice, can expose landlords to lease termination rights, rent escrow. Civil liability. We provide written records of the inspections and job that serves as evidence of landlord response to a rodent complaint.

How do you handle rodent treatment when tenants are occupying the unit?

We work with the property manager to give tenants required notice (usually 24-48 hours under NC law for non-emergency access). Job uses snap traps in inaccessible areas, inside cabinet bases, behind appliances, in crawl-space access areas. Outside tamper-resistant bait stations. We do not place inside rodenticide bait in occupied units. Tenants don't need to leave during job. Any pets should be kept away from the specific job areas during the visit.

Can you inspect a unit between tenants before move-in?

Yes, turnover inspections are one of our most-requested property management services. We inspect the vacant unit for droppings, gnaw damage, entry points, and any evidence of prior infestation. We produce a written inspections report covering the unit's current state, any active entry points. Said job or exclusion work before the new tenant moves in. This report protects you against future tenant claims of pre-existing infestation.

Do you work with property management companies that have multiple Greensboro properties?

Yes. Portfolio accounts receive centralized records across all homes, coordinated scheduling with a single point of contact. Steady service logs formatted for property management records. We serve UNCG-corridor multi-unit portfolios, mixed home-business portfolios, and single-family rental portfolios across Guilford County. Volume pricing is available for accounts with five or more homes under active service.
