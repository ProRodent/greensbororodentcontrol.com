Frequently asked

Common questions

What causes the Norway rat problem in downtown Greensboro restaurants?

The Elm Street and Davie Street restaurant corridor creates a concentrated Norway rat habitat through three intersecting factors: high organic waste volume from dense restaurant clustering. A shared alley system where dumpsters from multiple operators sit adjacent to each other and to storm-drain infrastructure. Building stock that dates to the early 1900s with basement-level and sub-grade utility access that gives Norway rats frame shelter adjacent to food sources. No single restaurant operator fully controls their exposure. The colony territory extends across the alley system and is sustained by neighbors' waste management as much as their own.

Will rodent treatment require us to close the restaurant?

No. We plan all restaurant job visits before opening or after closing. Inside snap-trap placement is in non-food-contact areas, inside gear kick plates, under dishwasher cavities, in utility chase access areas. Along wall-base travel routes. Outside bait stations are placed in tamper-resistant housings outside the building envelope. We provide records for your records before we leave. The only operational requirement is keeping staff away from job areas until the technician confirms placement is complete.

What does Guilford County Environmental Health look for during rodent inspections?

Guilford County Environmental Health inspectors look for evidence of rodent activity (droppings, gnaw marks, grease trails. Live or dead rodents), the presence of an active pest control service (service records and records). Frame states that allow rodent entry (gaps at door frames, utility penetrations, foundation gaps). Sanitation conditions that create harborage (accumulated grease, organic waste, improperly stored product). Having a current service log with bait-station inspections records and product records on-site at the time of inspections is the minimum records standard, we provide that with every monthly service visit.

How fast can you respond to a restaurant that just failed a rodent inspection?

Same-day. A failed health inspections is an emergency for a food-service operator and we treat it as one. Call (844) 635-0403, tell us you've had a failed inspections, give us the address and the citation specifics. We'll dispatch a technician and produce records for re-inspections as fast as possible.
