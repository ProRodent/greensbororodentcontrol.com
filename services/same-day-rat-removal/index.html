Frequently asked

Common questions

Which Greensboro neighborhoods get same-day rat removal?

All 27 Greensboro neighborhoods are in our same-day window. Irving Park, Fisher Park, Lindley Park, Downtown, Aycock, College Hill. All other Guilford County neighborhoods get a technician the same day you call in most cases, usually within 2-4 hours during business hours. Evening calls may be early next morning.

What's the difference between same-day and emergency rat removal?

Same-day removal means we plan and dispatch the same day you call, it's the standard for active rat infestations in Greensboro. Emergency removal adds after-hours dispatch and live-in-living-space response protocols. If you have a rat actively in your kitchen at 11 PM, that's an emergency call. If you found evidence this morning and need someone today, that's same-day.

Does same-day service include exclusion sealing?

The same-day visit includes inspections, trap placement, and ID work of entry points. For minor entry points with simple access, we seal same-day. For complex exclusion work, crawl-space foundation sealing, soffit-return replacement, gable-vent screening, that's usually scheduled as a follow-up visit 7-10 days after the population is cleared.

What should I do while waiting for same-day service?

Keep children and pets away from areas with visible droppings or rat activity. Don't try to block entry points yourself, sealing while rats are inside walls them in and creates worse problems. Don't set snap traps without knowing where to place them; mispositioning wastes time. The most useful thing you can do is note where you've seen activity, heard scratching. Found droppings, that info speeds up our inspections a lot.

How much does same-day rat removal cost in Greensboro?

We don't quote same-day rat removal by phone, because the price depends on the property. After a free inspection you get a written quote before any work starts.
