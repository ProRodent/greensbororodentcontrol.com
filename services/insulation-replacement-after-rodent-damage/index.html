When insulation replacement is necessary vs. cleanup only

About this service

When rodents move into the attic, insulation pays. We pull it out. We sanitize. We blow in fresh.

We start with a free inspection. We log all the gaps. We write up a quote. Then we work.

Written quote before any work.

  • Urine saturation depth: We probe the insulation at the highest-concentration areas. Surface saturation only (top 1-2 inches dry below), cleanup sufficient. Saturation through multiple inches, replacement warranted.
  • Nesting integration: Nesting material integrated throughout the insulation layer rather than sitting on top, replacement more efficient than trying to extract integrated debris while preserving insulation.
  • Frame compression: Significant R-value reduction from compression and redistribution, replacement restores thermal performance alongside hygiene.
  • Odor persistence after cleanup: If attic cleanup has been completed and odor persists after enzymatic work, the odor source is inside the insulation body and removal is the resolution.
