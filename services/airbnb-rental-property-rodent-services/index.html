Frequently asked

Common questions

How can a rodent in my Airbnb damage my Greensboro rental business?

A single negative review mentioning rodents is one of the most search-visible and conviction-driving review types on any short-term rental platform. Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com all surface negative reviews prominently. Unlike a noise complaint or a minor cleanliness issue, a rodent report triggers platform scrutiny, potential listing suspension. In some cases a required professional extermination cert before relisting.

How often should a Greensboro Airbnb host get a rodent inspection?

At minimum, once before fall season (August-September) when Greensboro's rodent pressure peaks. After any period the home sits vacant for more than 3-4 weeks. For homes in high-pressure neighborhoods, Irving Park, Fisher Park, Aycock, College Hill, Westerwood. Any home with heavy hardwood canopy, seasonal inspections twice a year (spring and fall) are the right standard. For hosts with continuous high-occupancy calendars, an annual full inspections plus between-guest checks when extended vacancies occur covers most scenarios.

Can you provide documentation I can show Airbnb if a guest reports a rodent issue?

Yes. Every inspections and job visit produces a dated, signed service report documenting the home state, any entry points found and sealed. Any active job deployed. If a guest reports a rodent issue, this records shows Airbnb that the home was professionally inspected and treated within a recent. Documented timeframe, which supports a host's dispute of a platform penalty or listing suspension. It doesn't guarantee a platform outcome, but it's the evidence a host needs to have.

What should I do if a guest reports seeing a rodent during their stay?

Call us immediately, (844) 635-0403. We handle between-guest emergency visits as same-day calls when availability allows, or first-available next morning. For the platform dispute, document your pest control response with a timestamped service record from our visit. For the guest, offer a good-faith partial refund while the situation is being addressed. Platform dispute records show better outcomes when hosts show immediate proactive response. Don't argue the report; address it.
