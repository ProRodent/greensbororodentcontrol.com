Locally owned rodent control in Greensboro, NC

Greensboro Rodent Control is an independent rat and mouse removal company at 2318 Summit Ave in Greensboro. We work Guilford County and the nearby Piedmont Triad towns, and rodents are the only thing we do.

Our story

Why we focus on rodents

Greensboro Rodent Control opened in 2022 with a narrow focus: rats and mice in Greensboro and the towns around it. Older homes with crawl spaces, tree-lined streets where roof rats reach attics, and busy restaurant blocks each create a different kind of rodent problem, and a one-size program misses most of them.

We stay close to home on purpose. Our service area is Greensboro, Guilford County, and nearby Piedmont Triad towns, roughly a 45 minute drive from our Summit Avenue address. Past that range we can't promise a same-day visit, so we say so on the call and point you to a company closer to you.

We also stay narrow on the work itself. We don't treat ants, termites, mosquitoes, bed bugs, or wildlife. Rats, mice, and roof rats get our full attention, which means every call starts with working out which species you have and how they are getting in.

How we work

What locally owned means here

Property-first inspections

Every call starts with a free, no-pressure inspection. We check the inside, outside, attic, and crawl space, identify the species, find every entry point, and write up a plan. You see the plan before any work starts.

Species-specific treatment

A Norway rat program differs from a roof rat program, and both differ from house mouse work. The technician on your job will tell you which species you have, how they are getting in, and why the chosen method fits your home.

Honest about what we don't do

We don't treat termites, ants, mosquitoes, bed bugs, or wildlife. If your problem isn't rodents, we'll say so and point you toward a local company that handles it.

No long contracts, no upsells

Home service is per visit or a short program, never a year-long contract. Restaurants, warehouses, and property managers who need recurring monitoring can set up a schedule, and that is a separate arrangement rather than the default.

Pest control rules

North Carolina structural pest control regulations

In North Carolina, pesticide use for hire is regulated by the NC Department of Agriculture & Consumer Services, Structural Pest Control & Pesticides Division. Its rules cover applicator licensing, label-compliant product use, and recordkeeping.

If your landlord, insurer, or health inspector requires specific credentials or service records, ask before you book. We will tell you plainly what we can provide for your property.

A lot of rodent work involves no pesticide at all. Trapping, sealing entry points, and cleanup depend on workmanship, and a sealed soffit return is only as good as the materials and the install.

Source: NCDA&CS Structural Pest Control and Pesticides
