Common questions

Is McAdoo Heights similar to Aycock or other historic Greensboro neighborhoods for rodent work?

Broadly yes, similar housing era, similar foundation vulnerability profile. McAdoo Heights is somewhat smaller-scale than Aycock (smaller homes usually, less elaborate architectural detail) so programs are moderate rather than extensive. The diagnostic principles and treatment methods are similar.

My McAdoo Heights home has had rodent problems repeatedly, what's likely the underlying issue?

Recurring problems on aged-housing homes usually indicate one of two underlying causes. First, prior treatment was trap-only without exclusion sealing. The frame entry points remain open for each subsequent population. Second, exclusion was partial, addressing visible entry points but missing hidden ones. Populations continue to enter through unsealed routes. Full inspections identifies which case applies.

Are McAdoo Heights HOA approvals required for exterior rodent work?

Most McAdoo Heights homes aren't in HOAs (the neighborhood predates most Greensboro HOA development). Homes that are in HOAs usually don't needs approval for standard exclusion work that doesn't alter visible architectural character.

Do you provide written reports for McAdoo Heights rental property work?

Yes. Rental homes get standard written reports documenting work performed, material used, and observed conditions. Format is right for landlord home file records and habitability records.
