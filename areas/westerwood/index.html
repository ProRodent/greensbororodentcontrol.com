Westerwood-specific questions

Common questions

Does my Westerwood home need different rodent treatment because it's a rental property?

The job itself doesn't change, same trapping, same exclusion, same materials. What changes for rental homes is the records. We produce written reports formatted for landlord home files, including dated evidence of inspections, work performed, material used. Any tenant-cooperation findings. This records supports NC habitability records and helps in tenant disputes if they later arise.

How can I tell if my Westerwood mouse problem will recur every year?

If exclusion sealing isn't done, recurrence is mostly guaranteed, mice find the same entry points year after year. Westerwood's compact lot pattern and varied foundation state mean exclusion is the only durable fix. Trap-only programs catch the visible population but don't prevent the next one from arriving through the same gaps. We discuss this honestly with homeowners. Budget constraints mean exclusion isn't now feasible, we'll do the trap work. You should plan for the situation to return.

Are there Westerwood blocks with more rodent activity than others?

Yes. The blocks closer to UNCG and Tate Street have higher rental density and slightly elevated baseline rodent call frequency. The blocks toward Fisher Park and the older inside portions of Westerwood are mostly owner-occupied and have lower baseline frequency. The difference is not dramatic, but it is steady.

Do you provide any heritage-friendly options for Westerwood Tudor and English cottage homes?

Yes. The decorative wood elements typical of Westerwood Tudor revival and English cottage homes warrant stainless mesh and color-matched flashing where outside exclusion work is visible. We default to galvanized in concealed crawl-space and attic-inside locations. Says stainless upgrade for any visible outside application on these architectural styles. Material upgrade is modest cost relative to total program.

What's typical cost for a Westerwood rodent program?

Around Westerwood, there is no standard figure for your property, because each property is different. We inspect first, then put the scope and the quote in writing before any work begins.
