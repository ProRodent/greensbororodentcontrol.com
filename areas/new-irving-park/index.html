Common questions

How does New Irving Park differ from Irving Park or Old Irving Park for rodent purposes?

Build era is the main difference. Irving Park and Old Irving Park are pre-1940 estate-scale heritage build with extensive roof rat and roofline complexity. New Irving Park is post-war mid-century build with simpler rooflines but still meaningful canopy contact. Programs in New Irving Park are usually less complex than original Irving Park but more in than purely modern suburban work.

Will my New Irving Park home need both crawl-space and attic treatment?

Sometimes. Homes with both crawl-space foundations and significant canopy contact may need both. Homes with slab foundations and limited canopy may need neither. Inspections determines what's really required at your specific home.

Does New Irving Park canopy management matter the way it does in original Irving Park?

Yes, where canopy contact exists. Six-foot clearance between any tree limb and any roofline element reduces roof rat delivery substantially.

Are New Irving Park homes more or less expensive to treat than original Irving Park?

Usually less expensive. Simpler rooflines, fewer architectural complications, smaller-scale homes typical here all reduce the per-home program scope. A typical New Irving Park program runs 30-50% lower than equivalent Irving Park work.
