Common Fisher Park questions

Common questions

If I only hear scratching in the attic, do I still need to check the crawl space in my Fisher Park home?

Yes, mostly for older Fisher Park homes. Roof rat activity is often more noticeable to residents because it occurs above living spaces. Norway rat activity in the crawl space is below and often unnoticed until inspections. Roughly 40% of our Fisher Park jobs that started as roof-rat calls turn out to have simultaneous Norway rat activity in the crawl space. Inspecting both is standard practice here.

My Fisher Park home is right on the park boundary, does that affect my rodent risk?

Slightly. Park-boundary homes see modestly elevated baseline pressure. Adjacent wooded park acreage supports rodent populations that sometimes migrate to adjacent structures. The effect is real but moderate, boundary homes usually face perhaps 20-30% higher long-term call frequency than inside-block Fisher Park homes. Exclusion sealing reduces this differential a lot.

Can a single program address both Norway rats and roof rats in the same Fisher Park home?

We handle both species under one program from the homeowner's standpoint, one inspections, one quote, one coordinated timeline, one final invoice. Operationally, the trapping and sealing work at the two elevations is independent. You don't pay for two separate programs. The combined cost is usually 30-50% higher than a single-species program, reflecting the larger physical scope, not double.

Do you carry stainless materials for visible Fisher Park exterior work?

Yes. Stainless mesh, color-matched flashing. No-drill attachment methods are available for Fisher Park homes where visible outside elements are part of the architectural value. We default to galvanized hardware cloth in concealed locations (inside crawl-space, attic-inside frames) where corrosion isn't a visual concern. Says stainless for visible outside elements. Material upgrade adds modestly to total cost.

What does Fisher Park dual-species program work typically cost?

There is no standard figure for your property, because each property is different. We inspect first, then put the scope and the quote in writing before any work begins.
