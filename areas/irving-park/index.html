Common Irving Park questions

Common questions

How can I tell if I have roof rats versus squirrels in my Irving Park attic?

Squirrels are usually active during daylight hours and produce loud, definite movement, the sound of an animal that can see and isn't trying to hide. Roof rats are nocturnal; activity peaks between 9pm and 3am and the sound is scratching, scurrying. Gnawing rather than thumps. Droppings tell you definitively: roof rat droppings are 1/2 inch, banana-shaped, pointed at the ends. Squirrel droppings are similar in size but blunter and more pellet-shaped. We confirm species during inspections rather than guessing from sound alone.

Will sealing my Irving Park attic require visible modifications to the home?

We design heritage-aware sealing mostly to avoid visible changes. Gable vents get hardware cloth fitted from behind so the original wood frame and visible mesh pattern stay unchanged. Soffit-return flashing matches the existing trim color and uses no-drill attachment where possible. Stainless mesh in visible locations replaces galvanized that would corrode and become visually obvious. Most clients can't identify our completed work from street level.
