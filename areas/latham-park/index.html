Latham Park questions

Common questions

Is Latham Park really a different neighborhood from Fisher Park for rodent purposes?

Yes, modestly so. Latham Park's smaller scale (smaller homes, smaller lots, less continuous canopy) produces somewhat smaller-scope rodent programs than Fisher Park usually needs. The diagnostic principles and treatment methods are the same. The differences are scope and total cost, Latham Park work tends to run modestly lower than equivalent Fisher Park work. There's less perimeter and roofline to seal.

How do I know if my Latham Park bungalow has crawl-space rodent activity I haven't noticed?

Indicators visible without crawl-space access: musky odor at floor registers or near baseboards, fresh droppings in low-traffic interior areas (closets, behind appliances), grease tracking along baseboards near suspected travel paths.

Are Latham Park homes typically straightforward fixes or do they end up complicated?

Most are straightforward. The smaller-scale, owner-occupied profile of the neighborhood tends to produce contained situations that respond well to standard programs. Complications are uncommon. The exceptions are usually homes that have had multiple decades without exclusion maintenance, those can need more full work. They're a minority of Latham Park calls.

Do you offer pre-purchase inspection for Latham Park bungalow buyers?

Yes. The inspection covers crawl space, foundation perimeter, attic, and visible exterior. The locations a general home inspection often touches lightly. Particularly useful for buyers new to century-old housing stock.

Pricing for rodent control in Latham Park?

There is no standard figure for Latham Park, because each property is different. We inspect first, then put the scope and the quote in writing before any work begins.
