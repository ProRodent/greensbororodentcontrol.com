Why coverage matters

What drives rodent pressure across the Piedmont Triad

Guilford County, core service zone

Our home territory. All 27 Greensboro neighborhoods plus High Point, Jamestown, Summerfield, Oak Ridge. Other Guilford towns are in our same-day window. Most calls dispatched within 2-4 hours.

Alamance County, eastern corridor

Elon, Haw River, Snow Camp, and saxapahaw sit on the Alamance-Guilford line, Haw River and the wooded corridors through these towns create year-round Norway rat and house mouse pressure. Next-day in most cases.

Rockingham County, northern reach

Eden, Madison, Mayodan, Stoneville, Stokesdale, and Ruffin. Rockingham's older housing stock and rural-edge homes drive crawl-space Norway rat and field mouse calls. Next-day from Greensboro for most addresses.

Randolph County, southern reach

Asheboro, Randleman, Trinity, Liberty, and Ramseur. Randolph County sits at the edge of our dispatch radius, next-day service standard, same-day possible for urgent calls. Asheboro is the most-requested address in this county.
